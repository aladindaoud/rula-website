A radical defense of embodied community in an age of frictionless isolation.

Why do modern societies boast unprecedented logistical connectivity yet suffer acute institutional detachment? In The Architecture of Belonging, Dr. Abdulrazak synthesizes seven years of ethnography across municipal centers, digital-first workplaces, and fragmented civic sanctuaries to reveal how physical and relational space dictates our capacity for collective trust.

The Disappearance of Frictional Space: Why Slowness is Our Last Civic Bulwark
