Institutional Cohesion Under Distributed Uncertainty: A Longitudinal Study

Principal Investigator: Dr. Rula Abdulrazak Co-Author: Prof. Henrik Lindqvist (Uppsala) DOI: 10.1080/jstp.2024.088219

Executive Abstract

How do civic institutions preserve internal coherence when operational mandates disperse across decentralized, non-state technological networks? Tracking 64 cross-sector civic bodies across the Mediterranean and Northern Europe over a six-year period, this treatise identifies three structural fault lines and models how epistemic parity acts as a resilient buffer against rapid institutional decay.
